## Chapter 1  
**Summary:** The narrator, a communist mole embedded in the South Vietnamese military and social elite, recounts the final days before the fall of Saigon. Moving between his role as a trusted aide to the General and his clandestine loyalty to the revolution, he helps manage frantic evacuation preparations while observing the panic, corruption, and selective mercy that decide who gets out. He frames his account as a confession extracted under pressure, emphasizing his divided self and the moral compromises required to survive on both sides.
