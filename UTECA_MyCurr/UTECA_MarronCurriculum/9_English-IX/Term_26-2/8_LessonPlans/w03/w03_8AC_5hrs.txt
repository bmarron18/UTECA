	Standard Model for linguistic Analysis (Konrod)
		Phonetics 
			==> language sounds
			==> phonemes, smallest unit of linguistic analysis, which are usually combined into words.
			==> articulation can change the meaning of a word
			
		Morphology / Semantics
			==> significant roles in constructing meaning (e.g., prefixes, suffixes)
			==> polysemy when the same word has several meanings
			
		Grammar / Syntax 	
			==> the set of rules that guide language users about ways to construct 
				meaning, language specific
				
		Discourse Analysis
