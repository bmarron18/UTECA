AI and Large Language Models
	What is AI? (slides)
	What are LLMs?


A Large Language Model (LLM) is a type of artificial intelligence (AI) designed to understand, process, and generate human-like text by analyzing vast datasets. At its core, an LLM functions as a probabilistic next-token prediction machine, determining the most likely next word (or part of a word) in a sequence, similar to a highly advanced smartphone auto-complete function.

Transformers: Modern LLMs are built on transformer architecture, a type of neural network that processes data in parallel rather than sequentially. 

Tokens: LLMs do not read words; they process "tokens," which are sub-word units or characters. These tokens are converted into numerical vectors (word embeddings) to allow the model to process semantic meaning mathematically.

Next-Token Prediction: The model calculates probabilities for all potential next tokens based on the prompt, outputting the most likely one.

Basic Prompt Engineering
a "prompt" to AI contains a Developer Message PLUS a User Message

Developer messages (AI role play instructions; system instructions)
	-gives the model high-level instructions on how it should behave while generating a 
	response, including tone, 
	- goals, and examples of correct responses. Any instructions provided this way will take 
	priority over a prompt in the input parameter.
		
  User messages (task instructions; input parameters)
		Ant text, image, or file inputs to the model that are used to generate a 
		specific response.

**	Intro to NM

Fall of Tenochtitlán
The Fall of Tenochtitlán on August 13, 1521, was the decisive event that ended the Aztec Empire and marked the beginning of Spanish colonial rule in Mesoamerica. After a brutal 93-day siege led by Spanish conquistador Hernán Cortés, the capital city was captured and largely destroyed, eventually being rebuilt as Mexico City.

Coronado Expedition
The 1540–1542 expedition led by Francisco Vázquez de Coronado was a massive Spanish quest for the mythical "Seven Cities of Gold" (Cíbola) in the American Southwest. Starting from CD Mexico, the army of over 1,000, including native allies, found no gold, instead attacking Zuni and Rio Grande Pueblo villages, wintering in Tiguex, and exploring as far as Kansas before returning empty-handed.

Juan de Oñate (1598) and Francisco Vázquez de Coronado (1540) were Spanish conquistadors who led major expeditions into the Southwestern U.S., driven by quests for gold and territorial expansion. While Coronado explored vast areas searching for the Seven Cities of Gold, Oñate formally colonized New Mexico, introducing livestock and establishing Spanish rule. 

Camino Real
El Camino Real de Tierra Adentro also known as the Silver Route,was a Spanish 2,560-kilometre-long (1,590 mi) road between Mexico City and San Juan Pueblo (Ohkay Owingeh), New Mexico that was used from 1598 to 1882. It was the northernmost of the four major "royal roads" that linked Mexico City to its major tributaries during and after the Spanish colonial era. Fue fundamental para transportar plata y mercurio, impulsando el desarrollo minero, cultural y religioso, siendo declarado Patrimonio de la Humanidad por la UNESCO en 2010.

%%% Major Themes %%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
1. Coming of Age
The novel is a coming-of-age story that follows Antonio Márez as he navigates his journey from childhood to adolescence. Throughout the book, Antonio faces various challenges that force him to grow and mature.

2. Identity and Cultural Heritage
Antonio is caught between different cultural and familial expectations. His father's vaquero heritage and his mother's devout Catholic and Luna farming background represent two distinct paths for Antonio's future.
 
3. Good vs. Evil
The novel explores the nature of good and evil, often through the lens of Ultima's healing practices and the presence of malevolent forces in the community.

4. Faith and Doubt
Antonio's journey involves questioning his religious beliefs and grappling with doubts about the Catholic faith he has been raised in.

5. Nature and the Supernatural
The novel frequently juxtaposes the natural world with the supernatural, emphasizing the interconnectedness of life and the spiritual realm.

6. Family and Community
Family and community play crucial roles in shaping Antonio's identity and values. The support and expectations of his family members and the broader community influence his decisions and growth.
