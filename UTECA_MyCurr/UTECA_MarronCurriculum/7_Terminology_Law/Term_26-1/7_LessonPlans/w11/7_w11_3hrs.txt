1. CONTRATOS TRASLATIVOS DE DOMINIO
	acuerdos que transfieren la propiedad de un bien o derecho de una persona a otra
		-compraventa, donación, permuta y mutuo. 

2. CONTRATOS TRASLATIVOS DE USO
	acuerdos donde una parte cede el uso de un bien a otra, sin transferir la propiedad, por un tiempo 
	determinado
		-gratuitos u onerosos
		
3. CONTRATOS DE CUSTODIA / contrato de depósito
	acuerdo donde una persona (depositante) entrega una cosa mueble a otra (depositario) para que la custodie y la devuelva cuando se le reclame, a cambio de un precio o retribución
	
4. CONTRATOS DE PRESTACIÓN DE SERVICIOS
	acuerdo legal donde una persona o empresa (el prestador) se compromete a realizar un servicio a cambio de una compensación económica, detallando las condiciones y términos del servicio

5. CONTRATOS ASOCIATIVOS
	acuerdo entre dos o más partes para colaborar en un negocio o empresa con un fin común, sin crear una 
	sociedad ni patrimonio separado, donde cada parte aporta recursos o actividades
	
6. CONTRATOS DE GARANTÍA
	acuerdos legales que aseguran el cumplimiento de una obligación, ya sea personal o patrimonial, y 
	protegen los derechos del acreedor en caso de incumplimiento por parte del deudor
	
7. CONTRATOS DE AFIRMACIÓN DE DERECHOS
	acuerdos que refuerzan o confirman derechos preexistentes, en lugar de crear nuevos, como la compraventa o el arrendamiento

** RFPs as precursors to Contracts
An RFP, or Request for Proposal, is a formal document an organization uses to solicit bids from vendors for a specific project or service. It details the project's requirements, scope, timeline, and budget, and invites potential suppliers to submit detailed proposals on how they can meet those needs. The primary purpose of an RFP is to ensure a fair, transparent, and competitive process for selecting the best vendor. 


Key components of an RFP
    Project overview: A description of the project or need.
    Scope of work: Detailed requirements and tasks to be performed.
    Timeline: Key milestones and deadlines.
    Budget: Financial parameters or constraints.
    Submission guidelines: Instructions on how to submit a proposal.
    Evaluation criteria: How proposals will be judged and the selection process. 

Why RFPs are used
    For organizations:
        They provide a structured and objective way to find the best vendor.
        RFPs encourage competition, which can lead to better prices and services.
        They help minimize misunderstandings by clearly outlining expectations.
	   
    For vendors:
        They provide a clear understanding of the project and what is expected.
        An RFP offers an opportunity to showcase capabilities and propose a solution.
        The process ensures a fair opportunity to win new busines

Marron's Rules for Consultants
-------------------------------
	1. NEVER give your client a draft. Only release your ABSOLUTE BEST WORK. 
	Remember, "God is in the details." (if you must break this rule, watermark every page of every 
	document with the word, DRAFT and state in the transmittal that the draft was per client request)

	2. Keep records of ALL client interactions (phone calls, email, casual conversations, etc. DOCUMENT 
	EVERYTHING!)
	
	3. Archive, Archive, Archive!
	
	4. Be real but absolutely professional AT ALL TIMES.
	
	5. Written communications must ALWAYS be at the highest level of professionalism.


A transmittal is a formal action. A transmittal is the act of sending your clinet your finished product. A transmittal includes:
	i) 	a transmittal letter; and
	ii) the deliverable(s) (your finished product)


The transmittal letter:
	+ a formal cover letter that accompanies a larger document or package, introducing the document and 
	providing context or instructions for the recipient
	
	+ standard business letter format, with a clear and concise tone
	
	+ explanations, instructions, new info (as needed)
	
	+ "Do not hesitate to call..." statement
	
	+ "thank you (appreciation)" statement (as appropriate)

	+ Triple-edited for typos and for accuracy
